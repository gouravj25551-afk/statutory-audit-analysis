Travel and Conveyance Policy
Policy Name: Travel and Conveyance Policy
Effective Date: 01-Apr-2024
Scope: Applicable to all employees claiming travel and conveyance reimbursement.

1. Eligibility: Travel reimbursement is eligible only for employees travelling on official duty.
2. Amount Limit: The maximum travel claim per trip shall not exceed Rs. 10,000.
3. Approval: All travel reimbursement claims must be approved by the Manager.
4. Timing: Travel claims must be submitted within 15 days of the transaction date.
5. Supporting Documents: Original travel bills and tickets must be attached as supporting documents.
