Entertainment Expense Policy
Policy Name: Entertainment Expense Policy
Effective Date: 01-Apr-2024
Scope: Applicable to client entertainment expenses incurred by the Sales department.

1. Eligibility: Client entertainment expenses are eligible only for the Sales department.
2. Amount Limit: Entertainment expense shall not exceed Rs. 5,000 per event.
3. Approval: All entertainment expense claims must be approved by the Director.
4. Supporting Documents: The itemised restaurant bill must be attached as documentary evidence.
